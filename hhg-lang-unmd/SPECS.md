## Step 3: Grammar and Syntax

### 3.1. Sentence Structure

The original sentence structure is **Subject–Object–Verb (SOV)**.

For example, the sentence *You talk to me*, based on the existing vocabulary, becomes:

> *Vaelith lenys comarr*

The more granular sentence structure is **Subject–Direct Object–Indirect Object–Verb (S–DO–IO–V)**.

For example, the sentence *I give you my lunch* becomes:

> *Lenys vaelith minuma tugs*

### 3.2. Cases

When a word or verb follows *to*, apply the following rules:

- If the word ends in a vowel, replace the vowel with the preceding letter, provided that the preceding letter is a consonant.
- If the word does not end in a consonant–vowel (CV) pair, simply remove the final vowel.

**Note:** For *in*, the case ending is **-s**.

When a word or verb follows *in*, apply the following rules:

- If the word ends in a vowel, put the previous consonant at the end of the word, e.g. comare &rarr; comarer
- If the word ends in a consonant, put the previous *vowel* at the end of the word e.g. conos &rarr; conoso
- If the word ends in a CC pair, add an -a between them.

### 3.3. The Verb *Conos* in a Sentence

For example, the sentence *You are in the firestorm*, based on the existing vocabulary, becomes:

> *Vaelith fulmoreth conas*

This is an irregular construction.

### 3.4. Conjugation

#### 3.4.1. Regular Verbs Ending in `-es`

**Present tense**

- **Present simple:** Remove `-es` and replace it with `-s`.
- **Present continuous:** Remove `-es`, replace it with `-eis`, and add *fin* before the conjugated verb.
- **Present perfect:** Keep `-es`, add `-a`, and place *nir* before the conjugated verb.
- **Present perfect continuous:** Conjugate the verb and place *fuin* before it.

**Past tense**

- **Past simple:** Remove `-es` and replace it with `-a`.
- **Past continuous:** Remove `-es`, replace it with `-eis`, and add *fan* before the conjugated verb.
- **Past perfect:** Keep `-es`, add `-i`, and place *nar* before the conjugated verb.
- **Past perfect continuous:** Conjugate the verb and place *fuin* before it.

**Future tense**

- **Future simple:** Remove `-es`, replace it with `-se`, and add *lin* before the conjugated verb.
- **Future continuous:** Remove `-es`, replace it with `-eis`, and add *fon* before the conjugated verb.
- **Future perfect:** Keep `-es`, add `-o`, and place *nor* before the conjugated verb.
- **Future perfect continuous:** Conjugate the verb and place *fuin* before it.

#### 3.4.2. Irregular Verbs

The verb **Conos** follows these conjugation rules:

**Present tense**

- **Present simple:** Remove `-os` and replace it with `-s`.
- **Present continuous:** Remove `-os`, replace it with `-ias`, and place *fu* before the conjugated verb.
- **Present perfect:** Add `-i` to the end and place *nu* before the conjugated verb.
- **Present perfect continuous:** Change the verb as required and place *funu* before it.

**Past tense**

- **Past simple:** Remove `-os` and replace it with `-t`.
- **Past continuous:** Remove `-os`, replace it with `-ios`, and place *fu* before the conjugated verb.
- **Past perfect:** Add `-e` to the end and place *nu* before the conjugated verb.
- **Past perfect continuous:** Change the verb as required and place *funu* before it.

**Future tense**

- **Future simple:** Remove `-os` and replace it with `-l`.
- **Future continuous:** Remove `-os`, replace it with `-eys`, and place *fu* before the conjugated verb.
- **Future perfect:** Add `-o` to the end and place *nu* before the conjugated verb.
- **Future perfect continuous:** Change the verb as required and place *funu* before it.

### 3.5. Possession and Vocabulary

The following sentence illustrates possession and indirect objects:

> *Lenys vaelith minuma tugs*

**Translation:** I give you my lunch.

The relevant vocabulary and grammatical rules are:

- **`-ma`** → My (possessive marker)
- **Tuges** → To give
- **Minu** → Meal (lunch, breakfast, or dinner)

### 3.6. Adjectives

- Adjectives appear **before the words they modify**.
- Adjective forms do not change according to tense, number, word order, or other grammatical factors.
- A basic list of adjectives is given below:
    * guri &rarr; good
    * nira &rarr; bad
    * col &rarr; average/normal (based on context)
    * mina &rarr; small
    * fira &rarr; big 

## 3.7. Articles

- Articles change for conjugation.
    * men &rarr; (root) the
    * len &rarr; (root) a/an

**Future tense**

**Perfect continuous**
* -n if it ends in a vowel,
* otherwise, -an

**Plural**: -f ending

## 3.8. Plurals

Verbs do not change for plurals. Nouns have one rule for pluralising, except for irregular nouns:
- put the second-last letter of the word (e.g. minu) at the end (e.g. minun), **only if it ends in a vowel.**
- if it ends in a consonant:
    - if it ends in a VC pair, do the same as the first rule.
    - if it ends in a CC pair, take the first vowel to appear in the word and append it to the end.
