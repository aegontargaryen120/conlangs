
# Dictionary

A vocabulary reference for the independent human language used in *The House That Held A God*.

## 1. Pronouns

| Word | Meaning | Part of speech | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Vaelith` | You | Pronoun | Second person |
| `Lenys` | I / me | Pronoun | First person |
| `Vaelas` | Yourself | Pronoun | Second person |
| `Nys` | It | Pronoun | <N/A> |
