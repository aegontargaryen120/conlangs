# Articles and Adjectives Reference

## 1. Articles

The language has two article roots:

| Root | Meaning | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `men` | The | Definite article |
| `len` | A / an | Indefinite article |

### 1.1. Article conjugation

Articles change according to tense and aspect. Apply the appropriate ending directly to the existing article root.

#### Present tense

| Aspect | Ending | Example with `men` | Example with `len` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | `-a` | `mena` | `lena` |
| Continuous | `-th` | `menth` | `lenth` |
| Perfect | `-a` | `mena` | `lena` |

#### Past tense

| Aspect | Ending | Example with `men` | Example with `len` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | `-i` | `meni` | `leni` |
| Continuous | `-io` | `menio` | `lenio` |
| Perfect | `-i` | `meni` | `leni` |

#### Future tense

| Aspect | Ending | Example with `men` | Example with `len` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | `-e` | `mene` | `lene` |
| Continuous | `-ei` | `menei` | `lenei` |
| Perfect  | `-e` | `mene` | `lene`

#### Perfect continuous

| Root ending | Ending |
|---|---|
| Ends in a vowel | `-n` |
| Ends in a consonant | `-an` |

Examples:

- `men` ends in a consonant, so its perfect-continuous form is `menan`.
- `len` ends in a consonant, so its perfect-continuous form is `lenan`.

**Note:** The specification gives one perfect-continuous rule without separate present, past, and future endings. Preserve this rule as written.

### 1.2. Plural articles

The plural article ending is `-f`.

Attach `-f` to the applicable article form.

Examples using the roots:

- `men` → `menf`
- `len` → `lenf`

The exact interaction between plural marking and tense/aspect endings has not yet been specified. Do not assume an ordering when combining them.

## 2. Adjectives

Adjectives appear **before the nouns they modify**.

Adjectives are invariant: their forms do not change according to tense, number, word order, or other grammatical factors.

### 2.1. Adjective vocabulary

| Adjective | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `guri` | Good | — |
| `nira` | Bad | — |
| `col` | Average / normal | Meaning depends on context |
| `mina` | Small | — |
| `fira` | Big | — |

### 2.2. Adjective placement

The general pattern is:

`Adjective + Noun`

Examples using established vocabulary:

- `guri minu` — good meal
- `nira minu` — bad meal
- `mina Fulma` — small fire
- `fira Molga` — big ice

These examples illustrate the adjective-before-noun rule. Their precise interpretation in a complete sentence depends on the surrounding grammar.

### 2.3. Adjective agreement

Adjectives do not change form to agree with:

- Noun number
- Verb tense
- Word order
- Other grammatical factors

For example, `guri` remains `guri` when describing a singular or plural noun.

## 3. Quick reference

| Feature | Rule |
|---|---|
| Definite article | `men` |
| Indefinite article | `len` |
| Present simple article ending | `-a` |
| Present continuous article ending | `-th` |
| Past simple article ending | `-i` |
| Past continuous article ending | `-io` |
| Future simple article ending | `-e` |
| Future continuous article ending | `-ei` |
| Perfect continuous article ending | `-n` after a vowel; otherwise `-an` |
| Plural article ending | `-f` |
| Adjective placement | Before the noun |
| Adjective agreement | No changes |
