# Conjugation Reference

## 1. Regular verbs ending in `-es`

| Tense | Aspect | Verb transformation | Auxiliary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Present | Simple | Remove `-es`, replace with `-s` | — |
| Present | Continuous | Remove `-es`, replace with `-eis` | `fin` |
| Present | Perfect | Keep `-es`, add `-a` | `nir` |
| Present | Perfect continuous | Conjugate the verb in the existing continuous form | `fuin` |
| Past | Simple | Remove `-es`, replace with `-a` | — |
| Past | Continuous | Remove `-es`, replace with `-eis` | `fan` |
| Past | Perfect | Keep `-es`, add `-i` | `nar` |
| Past | Perfect continuous | Conjugate the verb as required | `fuin` |
| Future | Simple | Remove `-es`, replace with `-se` | `lin` |
| Future | Continuous | Remove `-es`, replace with `-eis` | `fon` |
| Future | Perfect | Keep `-es`, add `-o` | `nor` |
| Future | Perfect continuous | Conjugate the verb in the existing continuous form | `fuin` |

## 2. Irregular verb: `Conos` (“to be”)

| Tense | Aspect | Verb transformation | Result from `Conos` | Auxiliary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Present | Simple | Remove `-os`, replace with `-s` | `cons` | — |
| Present | Continuous | Remove `-os`, replace with `-ias` | `conias` | `fu` |
| Present | Perfect | Add `-i` to the end | `conosi` | `nu` |
| Present | Perfect continuous | Change the verb as required | N/A | `funu` |
| Past | Simple | Remove `-os`, replace with `-t` | `cont` | — |
| Past | Continuous | Remove `-os`, replace with `-ios` | `conios` | `fu` |
| Past | Perfect | Add `-e` to the end | `conose` | `nu` |
| Past | Perfect continuous | Change the verb as required | Not specified | `funu` |
| Future | Simple | Remove `-os`, replace with `-l` | `conl` | — |
| Future | Continuous | Remove `-os`, replace with `-eys` | `coneys` | `fu` |
| Future | Perfect | Add `-o` to the end | `conoso` | `nu` |
| Future | Perfect continuous | Change the verb as required | Not specified | `funu` |

**Case example:** `Conos` → present simple `cons` → apply the `in`-case transformation → `conas` in *Vaelith fulmoreth conas* (“You are in the firestorm”). This construction is treated as irregular in the language notes.

## 5. Quick auxiliary reference

| Verb class | Present continuous | Past continuous | Future continuous | Perfect continuous |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regular `-es` verbs | `fin` | `fan` | `fon` | `fuin` |
| `Conos` | `fu` | `fu` | `fu` | `funu` |
| `Comare` | `fu` | `fu` | `fu` | `funu` |
| `Enetas` | `fu` | `fu` | `fu` | `funu` |

For regular `-es` verbs, the same `fuin` particle is intentionally used for perfect continuous across present, past, and future; the verb form itself changes according to the applicable conjugation rule.
