# Step 3 in the process: Grammar and Syntax

## Word order

* **\[Subject\]-\[Verb\]-\[Object\]**
* Direct object comes before indirect.
* Prepositions and other noun modifiers come before the noun.
* Subject, verb, and object depends on context and usage.
* Possessors come before possessees.

## Adjectives

* They go before the noun.
* If the adjective ends with a vowel and the noun begins with one, you add a *t* in between them, and the pronunciation is halted slightly, like a small pause.

## Affixes

* Negation:
    * The -far (consonant-ending words) or -ar (vowel-ending words) evolved from the word *dyfar*, which is used to negate a sentence.
    * *Dyfar*, when put at the end of a sentence, negates the sentence.
    * The suffix negates a word eg. *Cina* (rich) &rarr; *Cinafar* (poor)
